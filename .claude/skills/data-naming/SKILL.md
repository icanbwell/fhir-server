# Data Naming Standards

Read `docs/incantations.md` (repo-relative) in its entirety before naming or
reviewing the name of any table, view, column, or schema. It is the canonical source for
this repository's data naming standards (minimal translation, entity-based
naming, singular/plural conventions, primary key documentation, casing,
and acronym/abbreviation policy). Do not summarize or copy its content
here — always read the live page, since it may be revised independently of
this skill.
