# Engineering Conventions

Read `docs/conventions.md` (repo-relative) in its entirety before writing,
modifying, or reviewing code, or opening a pull request. It is the canonical source for
this repository's engineering conventions (dependency ranges, Databricks
dependency handling, canonical commands, test layout, Python style,
static-analysis policy) and the pre-PR checklist. Do not summarize or copy
its content here — always read the live page, since it may be revised
independently of this skill.
