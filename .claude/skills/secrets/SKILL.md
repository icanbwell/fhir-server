# Secrets

Read `docs/secrets.md` (repo-relative) in its entirety before adding,
retrieving, or deciding where to store a credential. It is the canonical source for this
repository's secrets policy (Databricks Secrets for per-environment
credentials, `decorative-secrets` for retrieval, 1Password for shared
non-production development secrets). Do not summarize or copy its content
here — always read the live page, since it may be revised independently
of this skill.
